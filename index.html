Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Contact: firstmaster.studio@outlook.com

Ludo First (“the App”) is published by First Master Studio. This policy describes exactly what the App collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to match the answers given in the Google Play Data Safety form and the Apple App Privacy questionnaire.

1. Identity and account

The App signs you in anonymously. We never ask for a password, and we never receive a phone number. What we hold is a randomly generated Firebase user ID. Anonymous guests can play all local modes and standard 1,000-coin public Online matches immediately.

You may optionally link a Google account, from the Profile screen, so that your progress survives a reinstall or a change of phone (and to unlock private Friends rooms, Team Up 2v2, higher entry-fee Online tables, and a one-time account-link welcome bonus). Nothing is linked unless you tap that action. If you do, Google Sign-In gives Firebase Authentication the email address of that account, and we hold it for that single purpose: recognising you as the same player later. It is never shown to other players, never used to contact you, and never shared. If you never link an account, no email address exists anywhere in our systems.

The only piece of information you write yourself is your nickname. It is visible to other players in lobbies, on the board, in quick chat and in friend requests, so please do not put personal information in it. Nicknames are screened automatically for offensive content before being saved.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No free text. In-game chat sends only a fixed list of preset phrases, cosmetic emote stickers, and lighthearted social animations (throwables). You cannot type a message, so no message content authored by you is transmitted.
  • No global online tracking. We do not publish a global online/offline status dot across the app; presence is tracked only while you are actively inside a multiplayer room.
  • No photos. The App cannot access your gallery or camera. Your avatar is chosen from a fixed illustrated cartoon set that ships with the App, and what travels is its number, never an image. Earlier versions allowed a picture from your gallery, kept on the device; that feature is gone, and the App erases any image left behind by it on first launch.
  • No blocked-players list on our servers. The players you block are stored on your device only. Blocking is per-device: reinstalling the App or switching phones clears the list.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera roll scanning, or payment details. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing (and the Apple App Store on iOS when released); we never see your card, address or billing information.

4. Purchases and virtual currency

On Android (V1), in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive a store-issued purchase token or order reference, the product identifier, its delivery status and the resulting virtual currency or entitlement (coins, diamonds, or No-Ads status). We use that information to deliver the purchase once and to reconcile later refunds or chargebacks. We never receive your card, billing address or other payment details.

Coins, diamonds, wheel spins, No-Ads status and cosmetic items are virtual goods with no monetary value: they cannot be exchanged, transferred outside the App, or redeemed for real money.

Account binding of the No-Ads pack (no_ads_starter): The non-consumable No-Ads pack is permanently bound to the Ludo First account that redeems it. If you voluntarily delete your account, your No-Ads entitlement is permanently forfeited and cannot be restored or re-claimed on a newly created account (see §8 and Terms of Service §3).

5. Advertising and consent

The App shows banner ads (on Shop, Inventory, and Social tabs), opt-in rewarded videos, and spaced short interstitial ads (after offline matches and every 3–4 completed online matches, never during gameplay, never on launch, and always suppressed when No-Ads is active or within 3 minutes after watching a rewarded video) through Google AdMob. Serving an ad necessarily discloses your IP address to Google — and with it the approximate region you are in — along with your device’s advertising identifier and how you interacted with the ad.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google-certified consent dialog (UMP) appears before any personalized ad is served. You can decline personalized ads and still play the game normally. If that dialog cannot be shown or is not completed, no ad is requested at all — the App does not fall back to serving ads without consent. Where the law requires a way back in, Settings shows an “ad privacy options” row that reopens Google’s form.
  • On iOS (when available), the system App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for your permission before any tracking identifier is used. Declining is fully supported.

Analytics is separate, and off by default. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in the App’s own configuration and is only enabled if you turn it on in Settings. The App sends no usage event before you do — including on first launch. The advertising-related consent signals stay denied either way; AdMob relies on the UMP choice above, not on this switch.

Crash reporting cannot be turned off. Firebase Crashlytics is always active and sends a crash report, your device model and your OS version when the App fails. We treat this as necessary to keep the App working, which is why it is declared as required rather than optional data.

6. Reporting and moderation

If you report a player, we record who reported whom, the room code, the preset message or emote concerned and the time. These records are used only to review abuse and to act on repeat offenders.

Because accounts are anonymous and therefore trivial to recreate, moderation records are kept even after an account is deleted — otherwise deleting an account would erase any record of abusive behaviour.

Since they outlive the account, we keep them to the strict minimum: two anonymous user IDs, a room code, one of the App’s preset phrases/emotes, and a timestamp. No nickname — it is the only text a player writes, and storing it here would have let a personal detail survive a deletion this policy describes as complete. The server refuses any report containing anything else.

The App contains in-app purchases and ads, and includes competitive matches played with virtual-coin entry fees. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us at firstmaster.studio@outlook.com and we will delete the associated data.

8. Deleting your account and data

To request deletion of your Ludo First account and its associated data, open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account, then confirm. This is immediate and irreversible. It removes:

  • your public profile (nickname, avatar, equipped frame) and your friend_codes lookup entry;
  • your wallet — coins, diamonds, wheel spins (spins), No-Ads status (noAds), XP and every cosmetic you own or bought;
  • your entire match history;
  • your friends list, including the entry that other players hold for you;
  • your pending friend requests, in both directions;
  • any matchmaking queue entry;
  • any virtual-coin entry fee held for you in a waiting room (for an in-progress unsettled match, the held entry fee is anonymized and detached from your user ID so the remaining players’ match prize is preserved);
  • your weekly login streak (weekly/streak), daily reward counters and your Lucky Wheel cooldown;
  • your in-room presence records (along with a short-lived anti-replay deletion tombstone);
  • if you linked a Google account, the Firebase Authentication record holding its email address — deleting the account deletes it too;
  • everything the App stored on your phone: nickname, avatar, equipped cosmetics, language, blocked players, unlocked history, and any offline game saved for later. Because the App creates a fresh anonymous account the next time you open it, this matters as much as the server side — without it, the new account would inherit the identity of the one you just deleted.

Permanent loss of the No-Ads pack (no_ads_starter): Voluntarily deleting your account permanently forfeits your No-Ads entitlement. Because the store purchase token remains permanently recorded against the deleted account for anti-replay protection, the No-Ads pack cannot be restored or transferred onto a new account.

Three categories are deliberately retained after deletion and are not linked to your nickname:

  • Moderation records (§6) — kept for as long as needed to keep a reported player from evading a sanction by deleting and recreating an account;
  • Purchase records — kept because they prevent the same purchase being delivered twice (including re-claiming a deleted account’s No-Ads pack), allow refunds and chargebacks to be reconciled, and because tax and accounting law requires them; these are retained for the period that law imposes (in the EU, up to 10 years for transaction records).
  • Advertising reward anti-replay records — deleting an account removes the user ID from its reward records. Only transaction identifiers and a hashed deletion marker remain for up to 30 days, to prevent a delayed advertising callback from recreating deleted data. Active reward records also expire after 30 days. Daily cleanup or Firestore TTL may add up to 24 hours.

If you cannot access the in-app option, you may also write to firstmaster.studio@outlook.com from any address to request deletion of your Ludo First account.

Game Rules

How Ludo First plays — the rules the game actually enforces.

Goal & variants

In every variant, each player starts with four pawns in their yard and races them around the board into their home column. Three variants are available:

  • Normal — bring all four pawns home to finish.
  • Quick (rapide) — bring one pawn home to finish.
  • Popular (populaire) — bring all four pawns home, with tactical dice rerolls: after rolling, you may reroll the die up to 3 times per turn at progressive virtual diamond costs (2 → 5 → 10 diamonds online; free in offline practice).

Getting a pawn out & extra turns

You need to roll a 6 to move a pawn out of your yard and onto your starting square. Rolling a 6 always earns an extra roll — there is no three-sixes penalty (consecutive 6s are unlimited). You also earn an extra turn whenever you capture an opponent’s pawn or bring one of your pawns all the way into the center home finish.

Moving & capturing

  • Move one pawn by the number rolled. If no legal move exists, your turn passes (in Popular mode, you get a short window to reroll before your turn passes).
  • Landing on a non-safe square occupied by opposing pawns captures all opposing pawns on that square: they return to their yard, and you earn an extra turn.
  • Entering the home finish requires an exact roll and earns an extra turn. Overshooting is not a legal move.

Safe squares

Eight squares on the track are marked as safe — the four colored starting squares and four star squares. Pawns standing on a safe square, in their yard, or in a home column cannot be captured. There are no blockades: multiple pawns sharing a square never block other pawns from landing on or passing through it.

Team Up (2v2)

  • Players sitting opposite each other are teammates: seats 1 & 3 form one team, seats 2 & 4 the other.
  • There is no friendly fire — you cannot capture your teammate’s pawns.
  • The team wins together once both teammates have finished (or both opponents have left).
  • Once your own required pawns are home, you keep taking turns moving your teammate’s pawns. In online 2v2, if a teammate leaves, their remaining pawns stay on the board for you to move on your turn.

Friends Rematch & in-game social interactions

  • Friends Rematch — after a private Friends match concludes, remaining players can tap Play Again Together to reset the same room and start a new round immediately using the same 6-character room code.
  • Quick chat & throwables — players communicate through preset phrases, animated cosmetic emote stickers, and lighthearted social throwables (tomato, applause, egg, rose — 50 to 100 virtual coins, rate-limited) sent by tapping an opponent’s avatar.

Turn timer & absences

In online matches each turn is capped at 35 seconds. If the timer expires, the server plays a move automatically and records a missed turn. Reaching 5 missed turns (cumulative — playing later turns does not reset the count) removes the player from the match instead of replacing them with a bot.
